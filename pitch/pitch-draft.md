# Pitch Draft

## AI Learning Companion for Vocational Training (FP)

We are building an AI Learning Companion that empowers FP students and teachers with personalized, accessible, and curriculum-aligned learning resources.  
Using M365 Copilot, AI ERP, and Generative AI, the solution simplifies complex technical content and supports real classroom needs.

### Problem
FP students struggle with abstract technical concepts.  
Teachers need fast, curriculum-aligned resources.

### Solution
An AI-powered companion that:
- Generates personalized explanations.
- Creates visual summaries.
- Produces exercises and activities.
- Supports teachers with didactic tools.

### Impact
- Better understanding for students.
- Time-saving tools for teachers.
- Scalable innovation for FP programs.
